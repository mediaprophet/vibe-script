The actual surface
A compact language, broad enough to meet the world.
These are implemented engine areas and shipped fixture families—not imagined product roadmap cards.

Cells & DAGs

Try a reactive program →

Animation & geometry
Ten animation preset families plus easing, springs, quaternion paths, PGA motors, and geometry fixtures.
Inspect the animation fixture →

        
03 / PHYSICAL
Fields, materials, laws
Quantities, units, physical fields, material properties, trajectories, and constraint-style laws are first-class.
See concise examples →

Graph & vocabularies
Canonical IRI terms, graph queries, bounded vocabulary chunks, provenance-aware terms, and policy-shaped data.

One source value can be coherently presented through visual, haptic, auditory, speech, and Braille forms.

It already does →
